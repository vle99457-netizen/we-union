## Design Specificity Verdict

The editorial-performance composition, monumental type, restrained gold, asymmetric layouts, and meaning-first journey feel authored for WE. The result is not category-interchangeable. Specificity weakens when the same three visuals are reused across worlds and when the customizer presents inert views.

The deterministic detector reported one `side-tab` warning in `src/styles.css:2296`; it is a false positive on an editorial blockquote. Source review found one real accessible-name defect in `src/components/ProductCard.tsx`, incomplete form constraints, and two mobile grid areas that require visual confirmation. Browser overlays were unavailable because the Playwright Chromium binary could not be downloaded in this environment.

## Overall Impression

The brand story and visual foundation are strong. The largest opportunity is to make the signature customization experience as trustworthy and stateful as the surrounding campaign design.

## What's Working

- The homepage sequence moves naturally from identity to worlds, originals, personalization, proof, stories, and community.
- The design system is cohesive across color, type, geometry, spacing, icons, and restrained motion.
- Prototype data, unavailable integrations, and unverified operational claims are disclosed clearly rather than fabricated.

## Priority Issues

### P1 — Customizer continuity and view controls

Product `?style=` parameters are ignored, templates do not change the visual, and Front/Back/Detail controls are inert. Hydrate from the URL, map templates to images, make view state real, and persist draft state.

### P1 — Mobile menu accessibility

The drawer closes on Escape but lacks initial focus, focus trapping, scroll lock, background inertness, and focus restoration. Implement a complete dialog interaction.

### P1 — Semantic and input hardening

Product image links can be unnamed; section heading IDs do not name the intended regions; small functional gold misses normal-text contrast; several form inputs lack constraints or meaningful names. Fix at shared-component and token level.

### P2 — Team workflow scope

The promised roster, proof, and tracking workflow currently stops at a contact form. Keep the current release honest and clearly frame it as a brief intake until the deeper workspace is implemented.

### P2 — Repetition and cognitive noise

Three source images carry too many roles, and repeated prototype notices interrupt premium reading rhythm. Preserve disclosures but consolidate them where possible; expand the approved shot list in a later content pass.

## Persona Red Flags
